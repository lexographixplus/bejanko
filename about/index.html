About

A place to think in public.

Mind Substances is the literary home of B.E. Janko Jnr. — a space for reflective writing, books, notes and ideas worth returning to.

B.E. Janko Jnr.

Writer, thinker and creator.

The work gathered here moves between the personal and the social: identity, responsibility, desire, attention, freedom and the questions people often carry quietly.

Mind Substances is intentionally smaller and calmer than a conventional media platform. It is designed around reading first: a place where an essay can breathe, a note can remain unfinished, and a book can sit beside the ideas that produced it.
